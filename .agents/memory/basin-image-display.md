---
name: Basin image display treatment
description: How to present the catalog’s mixed-background basin photos without damaging light-finish details
---

Use the supplied basin photos as full-frame, cover-fit presentation assets when background removal produces visible erosion or rectangular seams. Keep the original JPGs available as the recovery source.

**Why:** The light basin photos contain near-white product detail that is difficult to separate reliably from their pale backgrounds; aggressive flood-fill removal creates holes and splatter while full-frame presentation preserves the product.

**How to apply:** Keep the presentation frame consistent across catalog, hero, quote, Workbench, and Studio previews, and validate both a light and a dark finish visually after asset changes.
